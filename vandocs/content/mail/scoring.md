I don’t award points for looking busy. I measure whether the network works, how long it stays clean, and what you leave behind. **1,000 points available.**

| Points | What earns them |
| --- | --- |
| 600 service | Required workstation-to-server connections working throughout the match |
| 250 security | Hosts staying free of established infection throughout the match |
| 150 recovery | Hosts ending clean, online, out of isolation, and able to make their required connections |

Every tick counts equally. Required connections need clean, online endpoints and firewall permission. Routers don’t count as hosts. An infected host still counts as infected when it’s off.

The recovery figure on your dashboard is **provisional**. Leave a machine broken at the deadline and an earlier good screenshot will not help.

## The result on your record

- **Victory:** no infection or exposure, every server serving clients, and at least **90%** of weighted required connections working for **14 consecutive ticks through the deadline**.
- **Incomplete:** you reach the deadline without meeting recovery conditions.
- **Defeat:** no required connections work for **30 consecutive ticks**.

Recovery doesn’t end the match early. If those conditions stop holding, you lose the recovery streak. Early defeat earns no credit for the remaining ticks and no final recovery points.

Those are the defaults. A mission can publish different windows, thresholds, and required pairs. By default, every workstation needs every server.

## Before anyone proposes unplugging everything

Clean service throughout, everyone operational at the end: **1,000**. Isolate the whole network and you may stop infection spreading. You have also stopped the network. The service column will reflect this.

Selective quarantine, restoration, safe release. Do it early and you earn more useful ticks. Restore without protection and you may pay the downtime twice.

Compare **victory, then incomplete, then defeat**, and then points. Across several scenarios, compare victory rate, then average points. Use the same seeds and settings. There’s no bonus for using less fuel or finishing faster in real time.

[Read the required connections](/docs/network/). I score those connections, not your intentions. There is no fuel-efficiency prize tucked behind the service score.
